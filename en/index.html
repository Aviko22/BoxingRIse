What an hour with me looks like

Protect yourself

Today it's not just fitness.
It's confidence.

These days, more and more women want to know they can stand their ground. Boxing teaches your body to react, not freeze.

  1. 01

    Read your surroundings

    A steady stance, hands up, eyes open. Spot an uncomfortable situation before it gets close.

  2. 02

    Protect and make distance

    Cover your head, block and get out of the way. And know how to hit hard and right when there's no choice.

  3. 03

    Confidence people can see

    Standing tall, a direct look and a clear voice.

It's not a self-defence course, but it's a real foundation: a body that knows how to react, and a mind that doesn't panic.

08Is it right for my age and fitness?

09Can I come with a friend?

Yes. There are also duo sessions, arranged in advance. Message me on WhatsApp and I'll set one up for you both.

10How long is a session?

Sixty minutes.

11What if it rains or it's very hot?

If there's a covered spot nearby, we move there. If not, we reschedule at no charge.

12Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes, up to 24 hours before the session, by WhatsApp. Later cancellations count as a completed session.

13How do I pay?

Bit, PayBox or cash.

14What happens after the 8 weeks?

You continue at the pace that suits you: single sessions or a monthly plan. We'll plan it together at the end of the program.

15What if I need to stop midway?

You can pause the program once, for up to 4 weeks. If you need to stop completely, you get a refund for unused sessions, minus a cancellation fee.
