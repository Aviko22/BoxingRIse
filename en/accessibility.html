Accessibility statement

Last updated: October 2026

BoxingRise wants the site to be accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities. The site follows Israel's Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 5773-2013, and Israeli Standard 5568, based on WCAG 2.0 level AA.

What we've done

  • Full keyboard navigation, with a clear focus outline and a skip-to-content link.
  • Sufficient colour contrast, including gold text on black.
  • Alternative text for every image, and a clear heading structure.
  • Correct language and direction settings for Hebrew and English.
  • The device's reduce-motion setting is respected: animations are turned off.
  • Works on phones, tablets and desktops, and text can be enlarged to 200% without loss of content.

Known limitations

The background video at the top of the page is decorative only, with no sound and no information. If you run into a problem, we want to hear about it and fix it.

Accessibility at sessions

Sessions take place in public parks. You can arrange an accessible location in advance.
