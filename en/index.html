What trainees say

Before you start

Do I need experience?

No. Most people start from zero, and the first session is built for exactly that.

What should I bring?

Sports clothes, comfortable shoes and a water bottle. Gloves and pads are on me.

Is it right for my age and fitness?

Sessions are built around your level, so you don't need to be fit to start. Before the first session you fill in a health declaration. Children from age 10, with a parent's consent.

Where do we train?

In Yarkon Park, or in a park near your home in central Israel.

How long is a session?

Sixty minutes.

Do I have to spar?

No. There's no required sparring. We work on pads, technique and movement.

What if it rains or it's very hot?

If there's a covered spot nearby, we move there. If not, we reschedule at no charge.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes, up to 24 hours before the session, by WhatsApp. Later cancellations count as a completed session.

How do I pay?

Bit, PayBox or cash.

What happens after the 8 weeks?

You continue at the pace that suits you: single sessions or a monthly plan. We'll plan it together at the end of the program.

What if I need to stop midway?

You can pause the program once, for up to 4 weeks. If you need to stop completely, you get a refund for unused sessions, minus a cancellation fee.
