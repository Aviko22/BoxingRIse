Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

boxingrise.com is operated by Avi Anihovich (BoxingRise), who provides private boxing coaching. This policy explains what information is collected, why, and what your rights are, under Israel's Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981, including Amendment 13.

What we collect

  • WhatsApp messages: when you write, I receive the name and number shown in WhatsApp and the content of your message. The site itself has no forms and stores none of this.
  • Health declaration: before the first session you fill in a health declaration. I keep it privately for training safety, not on the site.
  • Usage measurement and cookies: the site uses Google Analytics to understand how many people visit, where they come from and what they click (for example the WhatsApp button). It loads only after you allow it in the banner, and only then are measurement cookies set. It does not receive the content of your messages, and data sharing for ad personalisation is turned off. Your choice is stored in your own browser and you can change it at any time under ‘Cookie settings’ at the bottom of the page.
  • Technical data: as with any website, the hosting provider receives technical browsing data, such as IP address and browser type, in order to deliver the site and protect it.

How it is used

To schedule sessions, answer messages, keep training safe and improve the site. Information is never sold or shared with third parties for marketing.

Third-party services

The site is hosted on Cloudflare, which processes technical browsing data in order to deliver and secure the site. If you allow measurement, usage data is processed by Google Analytics, subject to Google's privacy policy. Communication happens on WhatsApp, which is subject to Meta's privacy policy. See each service's policy for details.

Your rights

You may ask to see the information held about you, and to correct or delete it, as the law allows. Message me on WhatsApp at any time and I'll reply within 30 days.

Security

Information is kept on secured devices, and only I can access it. Health declarations are kept while you train and deleted on request.

Contact

Avi Anihovich, BoxingRise. WhatsApp: 050-273-4456.

Private boxing for women, one on one, in parks across Tel Aviv and central Israel.
