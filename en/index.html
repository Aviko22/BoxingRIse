What do you learn each week?
  1. Week 1 · Stance and movement How to stand, where your hands go, and how to move without losing balance.
  2. Week 2 · Jab and cross The fast punch and the strong punch, with power from your legs, not your shoulder.
  3. Week 3 · Hook and uppercut Punches from the side and from below, and the body turn that powers them.
  4. Week 4 · Combinations Linking a few punches into one flowing sequence on the pads.
  5. Week 5 · Defence Block, duck and step out of the way. Not only punching, also not getting hit.
  6. Week 6 · Defend and answer Defend and punch back at once. Where defence and attack connect.
  7. Week 7 · Boxing fitness Full rounds, with clean technique even when you're tired.
  8. Week 8 · All together Full rounds with everything you've learned, and a personal plan for what's next.

08Is it right for my age and fitness?

Sessions are built around your level, so you don't need to be fit to start. Before the first session you fill in a health declaration.

09Can I come with a friend?

Yes. There are also duo sessions, arranged in advance. Message me on WhatsApp and I'll set one up for you both.

10How long is a session?

Sixty minutes.

11What if it rains or it's very hot?

If there's a covered spot nearby, we move there. If not, we reschedule at no charge.

Yes, up to 24 hours before the session, by WhatsApp. Later cancellations count as a completed session.

13How do I pay?

Bit, PayBox or cash.

You continue at the pace that suits you: single sessions or a monthly plan. We'll plan it together at the end of the program.

15What if I need to stop midway?

You can pause the program once, for up to 4 weeks. If you need to stop completely, you get a refund for unused sessions, minus a cancellation fee.
