Flagship program

BoxingRise 8

From zero to confident, in 8 weeks.

Twice a week, one on one, at your pace. Each week adds one fundamental, and by the end you can stand steady, punch right and protect yourself.

  1. 01The basicsWeeks 1–2 · Stance and movement · Jab and cross

    Week 1 · Stance and movement How to stand, where your hands go, and how to move without losing balance.By the end of the week: You move in guard without thinking about your feet.

    Week 2 · Jab and cross The fast punch and the strong punch, with power from your legs, not your shoulder.By the end of the week: Your first punch that really lands on the pad.

  2. 02Power and flowWeeks 3–4 · Hook and uppercut · Combinations

    Week 3 · Hook and uppercut Punches from the side and from below, and the body turn that powers them.By the end of the week: You feel the power travel from your legs to your fist.

    Week 4 · Combinations Linking a few punches into one flowing sequence on the pads.By the end of the week: Your first combination, without stopping halfway.

  3. 03DefenceWeeks 5–6 · Defence · Defend and answer

    Week 5 · Defence Block, duck and step out of the way. Not only punching, also not getting hit.By the end of the week: You see the punch coming, and know what to do.

    Week 6 · Defend and answer Defend and punch back at once. Where defence and attack connect.By the end of the week: A fast reaction, without thinking.

  4. 04All togetherWeeks 7–8 · Boxing fitness · All together

    Week 7 · Boxing fitness Full rounds, with clean technique even when you're tired.By the end of the week: A full round without stopping.

    Week 8 · All together Full rounds with everything you've learned, and a personal plan for what's next.By the end of the week: You stand, punch and defend. With confidence.

After 8 weeks: You can stand steady, punch right and protect yourself, and you feel it outside the session too.
