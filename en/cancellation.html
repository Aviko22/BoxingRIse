Cancellations and refunds

Last updated: October 2026

Cancelling or moving a session

You can cancel or move a session up to 24 hours before it starts, by WhatsApp. Shorter notice counts as a completed session.

Weather

In rain or extreme heat we move to a covered spot nearby when there is one. If not, the session is rescheduled at no charge.

Trial session

The trial costs 49 ILS, paid on the day. You can cancel at no charge up to 24 hours before.

BoxingRise 8 program

  • Pause: you can pause the program once, for up to 4 weeks, for example for illness, injury, reserve duty or travel.
  • Stopping: if you need to stop completely, you are refunded for unused sessions at the program per-session rate, minus a cancellation fee.
  • Distance purchase: if the program was bought remotely (for example on WhatsApp, without meeting), you have the cancellation rights set by the Consumer Protection Law, 5741-1981. Cancellation fees will not exceed the legal limit.

Payment methods

Bit, PayBox or cash, paid directly to the coach, never on the site.

How to cancel

By WhatsApp: 050-273-4456. Any refund due is paid within 14 days using the original payment method.
